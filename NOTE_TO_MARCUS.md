# Note to Marcus

**What the tool shows you.** On Monday, the first screen answers your three questions: are we winning or losing in AI, against whom, and what to do. It shows our score next to Trakvia, Routelyne and Gridwell, why it moved, who took ground, false claims AI engines make about us, and up to five suggested actions. Behind it, Priya can see every question, engine and original answer, and export a board-ready Excel file.

**How the score works.** Each AI answer gives a company up to 100 points: 100 if it recommends us, 50 if it just names us, 25 if it criticises us, and 0 if it advises against us or leaves us out, a little less when we're named later. The score is the average across all answers, with the most important buyer questions counting more. AI engines answer differently each time, so scores naturally wobble by about ±10 points a week. The tool therefore calls a change "confirmed" only when it's bigger than that, and never compares a complete week with an incomplete one.

**Why I chose it.** It rewards what actually wins deals, being recommended and named first, rather than merely being mentioned. It's simple enough to explain in one sentence and to track every week. And it's honest about uncertainty: when a change might just be the engines' normal variation, it says so, rather than giving you a story that isn't there.
