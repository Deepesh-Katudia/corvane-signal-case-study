# Note to Marcus

**What you'll see.** On Monday, the first screen answers your three questions: are we winning or losing in AI, against whom, and what to do. It shows our score next to Trakvia, Routelyne and Gridwell, why it moved, who took ground, false claims AI engines make about us, and up to five actions. Priya's pages show every original answer and export a board-ready Excel file.

**How the score works.** Each AI answer gives a company up to 100 points: 100 if it recommends us, 50 if it just names us, 25 if it criticises us, and 0 if it advises against us or leaves us out, a little less when we're named later. The score is the average across all answers, with important buyer questions counting more.

**Why it moved.** AI engines answer differently each time, so scores wobble by about ±10 points a week. The tool calls a change "confirmed" only when it's bigger than that, and never compares a complete week with an incomplete one.

**Findings from the original six weeks.** Weeks 4–6 compared with weeks 1–3 show a confirmed fall of about 7 points for Corvane, while Routelyne rose about 12. Engines also state false facts about us: wrong headquarters, founding year, price ($45–49) and features.

**What I'd do first.** Correct the pricing and feature claims on corvanefleet.com, check the pages the engines cite, and brief sales.

**Why this score.** It rewards being recommended and named first, which wins deals, and is honest about uncertainty.
